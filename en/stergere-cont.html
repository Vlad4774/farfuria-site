Delete your Plate account

You can permanently delete your account and all its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Plate and go to Settings → Delete account.
  2. Type DELETE in the confirmation field and tap Delete account.

Optional: before deleting, you can keep a copy of your data (CSV) from Settings → Export data.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, write to vladbaracut7@yahoo.com from your account's email address and ask for deletion. We will do it within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, preferences, plans, favourites, shopping list and reviews are permanently removed from the servers. We keep nothing. A Google Play subscription has to be cancelled separately, in Google Play.
